2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PRODUCT VISION
================================================================================
This application is a user-friendly small-business financial management and
proactive advisory platform.

Users connect or grant access to locally stored business data (spreadsheets,
accounting exports, invoices, bank statements, reports) and fill in additional
structured information about their business (industry, entity type, tax regime,
location, goals, etc.).

The platform provides two tightly integrated pillars:
1. Core Small-Business Financial Services:
   Standard financial tracking and business health analytics—including revenue,
   income, expenses/spendings, cash flow, margins, trends, basic anomaly
   warnings, and baseline recommendations.
2. OpenShift-Powered AI & Governmental Intelligence:
   User data and holistic application context are analyzed by an AI model hosted
   on Red Hat OpenShift servers. Simultaneously, recurring automated jobs
   monitor official governmental sources for tax updates, new laws, compliance
   changes, and tax reliefs/grants. The system cross-references these external
   updates with everything the user has entered across the application to
   produce personalized recommendations, actionable solutions, warnings, and
   directly relevant legal/tax news.

UX PHILOSOPHY (MAXIMUM COMFORT & ANTI-FATIGUE):
Financial tools often overwhelm small business owners with constant alerts and
cluttered interfaces. A defining requirement of this application is a pleasant,
stress-free experience where users have full customization over personal
preferences—including the ability to turn off specific warnings, mute
recommendations, hide modules, set alert thresholds, and tailor the interface
for maximum personal comfort.

================================================================================
3. TARGET USERS & PERSONAS
================================================================================
- Primary Users: Small business owners, micro-enterprises, sole proprietors,
  freelancers, and early-stage startup founders who lack an in-house CFO or
  dedicated legal/tax compliance team.
- User Needs:
  - Understand business financial health at a glance without accounting jargon.
  - Reuse data they already store locally without painful manual re-entry.
  - Avoid missing tax changes, new regulations, or financial reliefs/subsidies
    that directly impact their specific business.
  - Receive concrete solutions and action items—not just raw charts.
  - Stay in complete control of what alerts and recommendations they see.

4. DETAILED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 LOCAL DATA ACCESS & INGESTION MODULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Allow users to securely connect or provide locally stored business
files to feed the financial engine and AI analysis.

Capabilities:
- Local Access Mechanisms:
  - Browser-based File System Access API (for supported browsers to grant
    scoped read access to a chosen local folder or set of files with user
    permission) with automatic fallback to standard drag-and-drop / multi-file
    upload.
  - Optional sync/re-scan trigger so updated local files can be re-ingested
    without recreating the workspace.
- Supported File Formats (Extensible Parser Pipeline):
  - Tabular: CSV, XLSX/XLS (bank statements, ledgers, expense sheets, revenue
    logs).
  - Structured Data: JSON, XML (e.g., local accounting exports, tax/e-invoice
    XML formats).
  - Documents: PDF (invoices, receipts, financial summaries, tax notices) via
    text extraction/OCR pipeline.
- Ingestion Workflow:
  1. File selection & format validation on frontend.
  2. Upload / secure transfer to FastAPI backend (with explicit user consent
     indicators explaining what is sent for OpenShift AI processing).
  3. Parsing, column mapping (auto-detected + user-adjustable), normalization,
     and deduplication.
  4. Ingestion preview & confirmation before committing transactions/facts to
     the user's business context.
  5. Management UI to inspect connected/imported files, refresh them, or
     permanently delete imported data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 BUSINESS PROFILE & CONTEXTUAL INFORMATION MODULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Capture qualitative and structural business details that raw financial
files alone cannot explain.

Data Captured (Editable Anytime + Guided Onboarding):
- Identity & Structure: Business name, legal form (sole proprietorship, LLC,
  partnership, etc.), founding date, fiscal year dates.
- Jurisdiction: Country, state/voivodeship/region, municipality (critical for
  filtering governmental and tax sources).
- Industry & Operations: Primary/secondary industry codes (e.g., NACE/PKD/NAICS),
  business model (B2B, B2C, SaaS, retail, services, manufacturing), employee
  headcount, contractor usage.
- Tax & Compliance Profile: Tax system (flat, progressive, corporate, lump-sum),
  VAT/GST registration status, payroll obligations, accounting method
  (cash vs. accrual).
- Strategic Context: Target profit margin, monthly revenue goals, planned
  hiring/investments, risk tolerance, pain points (free-text notes).

Global Context Propagation Rule:
- EVERY piece of data entered anywhere in the application (profile fields,
  local files, manual transactions, custom tags, goals, user feedback on past
  insights) must be aggregated into a unified "Business Context Snapshot" used
  by both the OpenShift AI engine and the governmental news relevance filter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 CORE FINANCIAL APPLICATION SERVICES (BUSINESS HEALTH ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Deliver essential small-business financial management features and
deterministic financial health analysis.

Core Features:
- Revenue Management:
  - Track gross revenue, recurring vs. one-off revenue, client/category
    breakdowns, and period-over-period trends.
- Income & Profitability Tracking:
  - Track gross income, operating income, net income (revenue minus total
    expenses), and profit margins across customizable date ranges (daily,
    weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly).
- Spending & Expense Management:
  - Track expenses, auto-categorize transactions (rent, payroll, software,
    taxes, utilities, marketing, supplies), separate fixed vs. variable costs,
    and allow manual edits/additions.
- Cash Flow & Liquidity Overview:
  - Inflows vs. outflows, net cash flow, burn rate, cash runway estimate,
    upcoming recurring bills/tax obligations.
- Business Health Diagnostics (Deterministic Baseline):
  - Composite financial health score/status (e.g., Healthy, Watch, At Risk)
    with transparent breakdown.
  - Automated basic warnings (e.g., sudden expense spike, declining margin,
    negative cash flow streak, high single-client revenue concentration).
  - Basic baseline financial recommendations (e.g., cost category review,
    reserve buffer targets) that work even before/without LLM inference.
- Visual Dashboard:
  - Clean KPI cards, interactive charts (revenue vs. expenses, cash flow trend,
    spending breakdown), and filterable transaction tables.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 OPENSHIFT-HOSTED AI ANALYSIS ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Leverage AI models deployed on Red Hat OpenShift servers to perform
deep contextual analysis and produce actionable guidance.

Workflow:
1. Context Assembly (FastAPI):
   - Aggregates the user's Business Profile, summarized financial metrics,
     detected anomalies, recent parsed document excerpts, relevant governmental
     updates, and active User Preferences (so muted categories can be excluded
     or deprioritized).
2. Inference Request (OpenShift Server):
   - Sends a structured prompt/payload over authenticated HTTPS/internal
     OpenShift Service networking to the hosted model endpoint (e.g., OpenShift
     AI / KServe / vLLM / containerized inference microservice).
3. Structured Output Parsing & Validation:
   - The AI returns strict JSON validated by Pydantic models in FastAPI,
     categorized into distinct actionable artifacts:
     a) RECOMMENDATIONS: Strategic, operational, or financial improvements
        (e.g., tax optimization eligibility, subscription consolidation,
        pricing adjustments).
     b) SOLUTIONS: Step-by-step action plans addressing specific problems or
        warnings detected in the user's financials or legal obligations.
     c) WARNINGS: High-signal risks (e.g., projected cash shortfall, approaching
        tax threshold breach, regulatory non-compliance risk).
4. Explainability & Traceability:
   - Every AI item MUST include:
     - Title & concise summary
     - Type (recommendation | solution | warning | legal_alert)
     - Priority/Severity (low | medium | high | critical)
     - "Why this applies to you" (explicitly citing user profile/financial data)
     - Recommended next steps (checklist format)
     - Source references (internal data metrics and/or official gov links)
     - Confidence/disclaimer note (informational aid, not licensed legal/tax
       advice)
5. Trigger Modes:
   - On-demand ("Analyze My Business" button).
   - Automatic after major local data uploads or profile updates (configurable).
   - Periodic scheduled analysis (e.g., weekly/monthly health review).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 RECURRING GOVERNMENTAL & REGULATORY MONITORING ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Proactively track official government sources for tax changes, new
laws, grants, and reliefs, and surface ONLY what impacts the specific user.

Capabilities:
- Source Registry:
  - Configurable list of authoritative governmental sources per jurisdiction
    (e.g., Ministry of Finance portals, official tax authority feeds, national
    legislative journals/gazettes, small-business grant & relief portals, open
    government APIs, official RSS/Atom feeds).
- Recurring Automated Checks:
  - Executed via scheduled jobs (OpenShift CronJobs or Celery Beat /
    APScheduler) on configurable intervals (e.g., daily or every 12 hours).
  - Polite, reliable ingestion: ETag/Last-Modified checks, deduplication by
    content hash/URL, rate-limiting, error logging, and retry policies.
- Processing & Relevance Matching:
  1. Fetch raw announcements/legal texts/RSS entries.
  2. Extract metadata via NLP/OpenShift AI: jurisdiction, effective date,
     topic (tax, labor law, relief/subsidy, reporting, VAT, grants), applicable
     entity types, applicable industries, revenue/headcount thresholds.
  3. Match processed updates against each user's holistic application data
     (profile + financial metrics, e.g., checking if the user's current annual
     revenue approaches a newly amended tax bracket or qualifies them for a
     small-business tax relief).
  4. Generate a personalized plain-language impact brief explaining:
     - What changed
     - How it specifically affects this user's business
     - Required actions and deadlines
     - Direct link to the official government source.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6 FULL CUSTOMIZATION & PLEASANT UX PREFERENCES ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Ensure maximum user comfort by making every warning, recommendation,
news stream, and UI element customizable.

Granular User Controls:
- Module & Insight Toggles (Master & Category Level):
  - Toggle ON/OFF: All Warnings, AI Recommendations, Basic Financial Tips,
    Governmental News, Tax & Law Alerts, Grants & Relief Notifications.
- Fine-Grained Rule & Severity Controls:
  - Minimum severity filter (e.g., "Show all", "Medium and above", "Critical
    only").
  - Custom numeric thresholds (e.g., "Only warn me about expense spikes above
    15%", "Warn when cash runway drops below 3 months").
- Per-Item Interactions:
  - Dismiss ("Not relevant"), Snooze ("Remind me in 7/30 days"), Mark as Done,
    and "Don't show recommendations of this type again" (permanent mute rule).
- Interface & Comfort Settings:
  - Dashboard widget visibility & layout customization (hide/show KPI cards,
    charts, news feed, AI advisor panel).
  - View mode: "Calm / Minimal" (clean, low-clutter summary) vs. "Detailed /
    Analyst" (full metrics and granular tables).
  - Theme & accessibility: Light/Dark/System mode, comfortable spacing/font
    size options.
  - Notification cadence: In-app only, daily digest, weekly digest, or manual
    check only (zero unsolicited interruptions).
- Persistence & Enforcement:
  - Stored in PostgreSQL (`user_preferences`) and enforced BOTH on the React
    frontend (UI rendering) and FastAPI backend (filtering API responses,
    suppressing muted notifications, and tailoring AI prompt instructions).

5. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & STACK SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 FRONTEND (REACT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core Framework: React 18+ (TypeScript strongly recommended for strict type
  safety across financial and AI response schemas).
- Build Tool: Vite (fast builds, clean static bundle for OpenShift Nginx/Node
  serving).
- Styling & UI System: Tailwind CSS + accessible component primitives (e.g.,
  shadcn/ui or Radix UI) designed with a calm, modern, high-readability palette.
- State & Data Fetching:
  - TanStack Query (React Query) for server state, caching, background
    refetching, and optimistic preference toggles.
  - Zustand or React Context for local UI/theme/layout state.
- Visualization: Recharts, Chart.js, or Apache ECharts for responsive financial
  charts (revenue/expense comparisons, cash flow, category breakdowns).
- Local File Integration:
  - Browser File System Access API (`showOpenFilePicker` /
    `showDirectoryPicker`) with graceful fallback to standard `<input type="file">`
    and drag-and-drop zones.
- Routing: React Router v6+.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 BACKEND (PYTHON + FASTAPI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core Framework: Python 3.11+ with FastAPI (fully async where I/O-bound).
- Validation & Serialization: Pydantic v2 (strict schemas for API I/O,
  financial records, preferences, and OpenShift AI structured outputs).
- ORM & Migrations: SQLAlchemy 2.0 (async via `asyncpg`) + Alembic.
- Data Processing Libraries:
  - `pandas` / `polars` / `openpyxl` for CSV/Excel parsing and financial
    aggregations.
  - `pypdf` / `pdfplumber` (or OCR adapter) for PDF extraction.
  - `httpx` (async HTTP client) for communicating with OpenShift AI endpoints
    and fetching governmental sources.
  - `beautifulsoup4` / `feedparser` / `lxml` for parsing governmental web pages,
    RSS feeds, and XML files.
- Authentication & Security:
  - OAuth2 with JWT (access + refresh tokens), password hashing (`argon2` or
    `bcrypt`), CORS restrictions, per-user tenant isolation on all queries.
- Background Jobs & Scheduling:
  - OpenShift CronJobs (preferred for cloud-native scheduled gov-source checks
    and periodic AI analysis) and/or Celery + Redis / FastAPI BackgroundTasks
    for async file processing and long-running AI inference jobs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 INFRASTRUCTURE & AI DEPLOYMENT (RED HAT OPENSHIFT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Containerization:
  - Multi-stage Dockerfiles / Containerfiles compatible with OpenShift's
    non-root arbitrary UID security constraints (strictly no root-only runtime
    assumptions; writable temp/cache dirs group-writable by root group `0`).
- Workloads on OpenShift:
  - `frontend` Deployment + Service + TLS Route (Nginx serving React build).
  - `backend` Deployment + Service + TLS Route (FastAPI via Uvicorn/Gunicorn).
  - `ai-model-service` (Red Hat OpenShift AI / KServe / vLLM / custom model
    serving pod exposing an internal REST/OpenAI-compatible inference API).
  - `gov-monitor-cronjob` OpenShift CronJob running the Python regulatory
    scraper/poller script on a schedule.
  - `postgres` StatefulSet / Managed PostgreSQL (with `pgvector` extension if
    semantic RAG matching is enabled for legal documents and user files).
  - `redis` Deployment (optional, for job queues, rate limiting, and caching).
- Configuration & Secrets:
  - OpenShift ConfigMaps for non-sensitive settings (source URLs, intervals,
    model endpoint hostnames).
  - OpenShift Secrets for DB credentials, JWT signing keys, and AI API tokens.
- Observability:
  - Health probes (`/health/live`, `/health/ready`) on FastAPI.
  - Structured JSON logging for OpenShift log aggregation.

7. CORE DATA MODELS (CONCEPTUAL DATABASE SCHEMA)
================================================================================

1. User
   - id (UUID, PK), email (unique), hashed_password, full_name, created_at,
     is_active

2. BusinessProfile
   - id (UUID, PK), user_id (FK -> User)
   - business_name, legal_form, country_code, region, city
   - industry_code, industry_description, business_model
   - employee_count, tax_regime, vat_registered (bool), currency_code
   - fiscal_year_start_month, goals_json, additional_context_notes (text)
   - updated_at

3. DataSourceFile
   - id (UUID, PK), user_id (FK -> User)
   - filename, file_type (csv|xlsx|pdf|xml|json), source_mode (local_sync|upload)
   - content_hash, status (pending|parsed|failed), row_count
   - extracted_summary (text/json), uploaded_at, last_synced_at

4. FinancialTransaction
   - id (UUID, PK), user_id (FK -> User), source_file_id (FK, nullable)
   - transaction_date, type (revenue|expense|tax|transfer)
   - amount (Numeric/Decimal), currency_code
   - category, subcategory, counterparty, description
   - is_recurring (bool), metadata_json, created_at

5. FinancialHealthSnapshot
   - id (UUID, PK), user_id (FK -> User), period_start, period_end
   - total_revenue, total_expenses, net_income, profit_margin_pct
   - cash_flow_net, burn_rate, estimated_runway_months
   - health_score (0-100), metrics_json, computed_at

6. GovSource
   - id (UUID, PK), name, country_code, region (nullable)
   - source_url, source_type (rss|api|html_scrape), topics_array
   - check_interval_hours, last_checked_at, is_active

7. GovUpdateItem
   - id (UUID, PK), gov_source_id (FK -> GovSource)
   - title, official_url, published_at, effective_date (nullable)
   - content_hash (unique), raw_text, ai_summary
   - tags_json (tax_types, industries, entity_types, reliefs_or_grants)
   - embedding (vector, optional via pgvector), fetched_at

8. InsightItem (Unified Recommendations, Solutions, Warnings & Legal Impacts)
   - id (UUID, PK), user_id (FK -> User)
   - insight_type (recommendation|solution|warning|gov_legal_impact)
   - origin (openshift_ai|deterministic_finance|gov_monitor)
   - category (tax|law|relief|cashflow|spending|revenue|operations)
   - severity (low|medium|high|critical)
   - title, summary, relevance_explanation, action_steps_json
   - source_references_json (links to GovUpdateItem or financial metrics)
   - status (active|done|dismissed|snoozed), snoozed_until (nullable)
   - created_at

9. UserPreferences
   - id (UUID, PK), user_id (FK -> User, unique)
   - enabled_modules_json:
       { warnings: true, recommendations: true, solutions: true,
         gov_news: true, tax_reliefs: true, basic_financial_tips: true }
   - min_warning_severity (low|medium|high|critical)
   - custom_thresholds_json:
       { expense_spike_pct: 15, min_runway_months: 3, margin_drop_pct: 5 }
   - muted_categories_array (list of strings)
   - muted_rule_keys_array (list of specific suppressed warning/tip IDs)
   - dashboard_layout_json (widget visibility and ordering)
   - ui_comfort_json:
       { theme: "system", view_mode: "calm", notification_cadence: "weekly" }
   - updated_at

9. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS & BEST PRACTICES
================================================================================
1. Security & Data Privacy:
   - TLS/HTTPS everywhere; strict user-level authorization checks on every
     endpoint so no tenant can view another business's financial records.
   - Sensitive financial files must never be logged in plaintext.
   - Explicit transparency in UI regarding what data is transmitted to the
     OpenShift AI model server, with full user data deletion capability.
2. Reliability & Graceful Degradation:
   - If the OpenShift AI model endpoint is slow or temporarily unavailable,
     core financial tracking and deterministic health warnings MUST continue
     functioning normally with clear, calm status feedback.
   - If an external government website changes layout or times out, the monitor
     must fail gracefully per-source without blocking other sources.
3. Compliance & Disclaimers:
   - Clearly label AI-generated tax, legal, and financial recommendations as
     informational decision-support tools—not certified CPA or legal advice—and
     always link directly to official governmental source publications.
4. Performance & UX Polish:
   - Financial calculations must use exact decimal arithmetic (`Decimal` in
     Python), never floating-point rounding errors for currency.
   - Long-running AI analyses run asynchronously so the UI remains responsive.
   - Zero dark patterns, zero nagging modals; honor every "turn off" toggle
     immediately and persistently.
